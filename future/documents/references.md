# External references

Status: Draft

## Purpose

The References document keeps useful external links and brief context so
Direction can revisit evidence without relying on conversation history.

## Ownership and location

- Direction creates one optional `.workflow/references.md` when the first
  relevant external link appears.
- Direction records relevant links supplied by the user or found during its
  investigation.
- Other workflow steps may read and link to the register.

## Entries

Each entry has a clear title, the original URL, and a short explanation of why
the source matters to the project. Link to a Direction topic or open question
when that relationship helps a future reader. Mark an important claim as
unverified when the source has not been checked. Preserve a useful user link
even when its effect on Direction is not yet known.

The register is a source index, not authority for the project's intended end
state. A source may inform a Direction document or PDR, but that document owns
the resulting conclusion. Do not copy long source passages or research reports
into the register.

## Maintenance

Add a relevant link during the discussion, before moving to another major
topic. Merge duplicate entries. Revise an entry when its relevance or known
limits change. Remove a link only when it is no longer relevant, and update
inbound links. Group entries by topic when that helps navigation.

Keep each entry brief and informative. No hard word limit applies to the whole
register. Prune duplicates and obsolete entries before adding structure.
